Questions

Good to know

Do the bonuses cost anything?

No. They're a free gift for readers of The Living Christian Vision Board 2027.

What paper size are the printables?

US Letter (8.5 × 11 in). If you print on A4, choose "Fit to page" in your printer settings.

Can I print them more than once?

Yes, as many times as you like for your own personal use. Please don't resell or share the files.

The email hasn't arrived. What now?

Give it a couple of minutes and check your Spam or Promotions folder. If it's still missing, sign up again making sure your email is spelled correctly.

How do I set a wallpaper on my phone?

Open the image on your phone, save it to your photos, then choose it in Settings > Wallpaper (iPhone) or Settings > Display > Wallpaper (Android).
